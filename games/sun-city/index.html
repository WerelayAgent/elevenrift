About this game

Sun City is a tactical 5v5 shooter that runs in your browser. Two teams fight over the grid that keeps the city running, one round at a time. There is nothing to install; you need a keyboard and a mouse.

Modes

  • Sabotage: 5 against 5 on two sites, first team to 7 rounds wins. Plan your route, buy, then plant or defuse. 10 seconds to buy, 100-second rounds.
  • Blitz: the same goal with faster movement, a double jump and a dash on Q. 6 seconds to buy, 60-second rounds.
  • Deathmatch: free for all with 10 players. You respawn in seconds and every gun is free. 10 minutes or first to 30 kills.

Buying and weapons

At the start of a round press B to open the buy menu: armour, sidearms, SMGs, rifles and utility such as smokes and decoys. The Spark pistol and the Seam knife are always with you. The Tap SMG, the Busbar rifle and the scoped Parallax are bought with the round's money, so every round you decide what to spend and what to save.

Cases and finishes

Weapon finishes come from cases. The Lunar, Orbit and Solar cases each open with a key of the same name, and every case shows what it can drop before you open it. The odds are 75 / 20 / 4 / 0.7 %, and some cases add a rare special item at 0.3 %. Rarities go from Service and Workshop up to Relic and Gold.

A finish you get is saved in your inventory. Equip it, keep it, or sell it.
